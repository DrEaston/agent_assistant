Listen for

These answers help you judge whether the role matches the work you want.

  • Architecture maturity: named AWS services, clear ownership boundaries, and a realistic migration sequence.
  • Data product maturity: conformed models, governed metrics, and a plan for usable analytics rather than only ingestion.
  • AI seriousness: specific customer or internal workflows where ML could be deployed, not just broad enthusiasm.
  • Role clarity: concrete first projects, success measures, and how much time is spent building versus coordinating.
