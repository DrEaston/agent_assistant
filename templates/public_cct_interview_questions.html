Technical areas to explore

These are the areas where the project research suggests the role could create value.

Data platform foundation

  • Customer database ingestion from on-prem environments into AWS.
  • Raw landing zones, transformation jobs, data quality checks, and repeatable migration patterns.
  • Conformed customer, machine, transaction, promotion, and property-level models.

Analytics and modeling

  • Customer return prediction, visit frequency, time-until-next-visit, and player value models.
  • Machine revenue forecasting, underperformance detection, failure prediction, and floor optimization.
  • Forecasting for cash demand, revenue, staffing, denomination demand, and operational planning.

Production readiness

  • Model targets, observation grains, prediction horizons, leakage controls, and evaluation metrics.
  • Explainability and business trust for operational users.
  • Deployment cadence, monitoring, and feedback loops from casino operations.

What strong answers would clarify

Strong answers should make the migration sequence, data product goals, and ownership model more concrete.

  • Clear migration state: which workloads have moved, which remain on-prem, and what is blocking the next phase.
  • Coherent architecture: named storage, transformation, warehouse, orchestration, and semantic-layer choices.
  • Practical AI roadmap: specific use cases tied to centralized data, evaluation, deployment, and customer value.
  • Defined role scope: concrete first projects, decision rights, team interfaces, and 6–12 month success criteria.
