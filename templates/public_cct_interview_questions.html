CCT product map

Technical opportunities organized around CCT's own Casino Insight product areas.

Product labels and functional areas are summarized from CCT's public site: Casino Insight, Insight Cash, and Insight Intelligence.

Casino Insight is the umbrella

The useful roadmap units are the product areas underneath it. Casino Insight provides the shared platform context across casino operations, finance, audit, marketing, and hospitality.

  • Executives & Finance
  • Cage & Vault
  • Audit & Compliance
  • Slots & Tables
  • Marketing & Players
  • Hotel & F&B
Insight Cash Cage, vault, audit, reconciliation

Core relationship

  • Insight Cash appears closest to the operational-control center of the product suite.
  • It connects cage, vault, kiosk, audit, compliance, and accounting workflows.
  • CCT emphasizes many integrations, configurable workflows, variance tracking, transaction history, and journal-entry support.

Likely technical work

  • Ingest and normalize cash, kiosk, cage, vault, and accounting events from many source systems.
  • Build reconciliation rules, variance detection, audit lineage, and exception workflows.
  • Support traceable transaction history and downstream finance/compliance reporting.

Future analytics / AI

  • Predict cash demand, kiosk refill needs, and abnormal variance patterns.
  • Prioritize exceptions by risk, operational impact, and likely root cause.
  • Use explainable anomaly detection before considering more complex models.
Insight Analytics KPIs, operations, customers, machine performance

Core relationship

  • Insight Analytics is the reporting and KPI layer across gaming and non-gaming operations.
  • CCT highlights operating reports, performance views, customizable KPIs, and cross-department analysis.
  • This is where the data platform becomes visible to executives, operators, marketing, and finance teams.

Likely technical work

  • Conform source data into consistent grains for player, machine, transaction, promotion, and department-level analysis.
  • Build star schemas, metric catalogs, data quality checks, and role-specific dashboards.
  • Separate raw ingestion from trusted semantic models so reports are explainable and repeatable.

Future analytics / AI

  • Customer return prediction, visit frequency, time-until-next-visit, and player value modeling.
  • Machine underperformance detection, floor performance analysis, and conversion recommendations.
  • Promotion response and uplift modeling once campaign and outcome data are reliable.
Insight Intelligence Predictive models and operational forecasting

Core relationship

  • CCT describes Insight Intelligence as its predictive product direction.
  • Kiosk Intelligence is presented as live, with Player Intelligence and Operational Intelligence listed as coming next.
  • This product area depends most directly on the quality of the underlying platform and historical data.

Likely technical work

  • Define targets, observation grains, horizons, leakage controls, and evaluation metrics for every model.
  • Create reusable feature pipelines from the same conformed models that support analytics.
  • Monitor model drift, prediction quality, and operational adoption after deployment.

Future analytics / AI

  • Kiosk cash forecasting and refill optimization.
  • Player return, value, segmentation, and next-best-action style recommendations.
  • Operational forecasts for staffing, revenue, denomination demand, and exception prioritization.

What strong answers would clarify

Strong answers should make the migration sequence, data product goals, and ownership model more concrete.

  • Clear migration state: which workloads have moved, which remain on-prem, and what is blocking the next phase.
  • Coherent architecture: named storage, transformation, warehouse, orchestration, and semantic-layer choices.
  • Practical AI roadmap: specific use cases tied to centralized data, evaluation, deployment, and customer value.
  • Defined role scope: concrete first projects, decision rights, team interfaces, and 6–12 month success criteria.
