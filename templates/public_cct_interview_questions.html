CCT technical brief

Cloud migration, data platform, analytics, and practical AI opportunities

A working summary of the likely technical situation, the kinds of problems the role may need to solve, and the questions worth clarifying with CCT leadership.

Working understanding

This is framed as a hypothesis from the project research, not as a claim about CCT's internal roadmap.

Likely migration status

CCT appears to be moving from customer-specific, on-prem SQL environments toward a more centralized AWS data platform. The remaining work is likely a mix of ingestion, migration sequencing, data standardization, and building trusted analytics layers on top of the new platform.

Likely need from the role

The role will probably need to bridge infrastructure and analytics: reliable data pipelines, transformation layers, conformed models, semantic definitions, and pragmatic ML/AI use cases that can be supported by the platform once the data foundation is stable.

Likely technical problems

The hard problems are probably data consistency across customers, historical migration from SQL Server systems, entity resolution, schema drift, metric governance, temporal leakage in models, and making casino operations data usable for forecasting, recommendations, and exception detection.

Likely product opportunity

Once centralized data is trustworthy, CCT may be able to build stronger customer analytics, machine performance analytics, cash/staffing/revenue forecasting, and decision-support tools for casino operators.

Technical areas to explore

These are the areas where the project research suggests the role could create value.

Data platform foundation

  • Customer database ingestion from on-prem environments into AWS.
  • Raw landing zones, transformation jobs, data quality checks, and repeatable migration patterns.
  • Conformed customer, machine, transaction, promotion, and property-level models.

Analytics and modeling

  • Customer return prediction, visit frequency, time-until-next-visit, and player value models.
  • Machine revenue forecasting, underperformance detection, failure prediction, and floor optimization.
  • Forecasting for cash demand, revenue, staffing, denomination demand, and operational planning.

Production readiness

  • Model targets, observation grains, prediction horizons, leakage controls, and evaluation metrics.
  • Explainability and business trust for operational users.
  • Deployment cadence, monitoring, and feedback loops from casino operations.

What strong answers would clarify

Strong answers should make the migration sequence, data product goals, and ownership model more concrete.

  • Clear migration state: which workloads have moved, which remain on-prem, and what is blocking the next phase.
  • Coherent architecture: named storage, transformation, warehouse, orchestration, and semantic-layer choices.
  • Practical AI roadmap: specific use cases tied to centralized data, evaluation, deployment, and customer value.
  • Defined role scope: concrete first projects, decision rights, team interfaces, and 6–12 month success criteria.
