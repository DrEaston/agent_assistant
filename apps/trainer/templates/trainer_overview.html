Strides are found in each run's second-by-second speed from Strava: short bursts (6 to 60 seconds) well above that run's usual pace. Stride speed is how fast they are at their best 5 seconds, the middle of the week's strides. It's the same relaxed-fast effort each time, so when that pace drops, you're getting faster. Top speed is the fastest moment GPS saw on your runs (one GPS glitch can set it). Aerobic efficiency is easy-run speed per heartbeat (metres a minute ÷ heart rate): when it goes up, the same effort is getting you further.
