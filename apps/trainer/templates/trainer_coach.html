What they'll get

    How Dieter sets the paces
    • Each athlete gets their own times. Efforts like threshold, 5K or mile pace come from that athlete's runs and races. You never see their times; they see theirs under each set.
    • Sprint depends on rep length. Reps up to 150 m (strides, flys) are near-max. Longer "sprint" reps are run at race pace for the set's total distance: 4 × 200 is run at 800 m race pace, 8 × 400 at 3K pace. That's fast, but they can hold it for every rep.
    • It's fitted to their week. Before an athlete takes a workout, Dieter adds up how much fast running it is against their safe limit for that week (it rises with the speed work they've been doing). If it won't fit, Dieter eases the fast reps, only as much as needed, and you'll see Eased for their limits once they take it.
    • Reps and rounds are never cut. If easing the pace isn't enough, the athlete sees that the session is too much for their week, and it's up to the two of you.

    Dieter works out each athlete's own paces for every set from their runs.
