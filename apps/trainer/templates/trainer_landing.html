What it shows you

  • Pace heat maps Every route coloured from easy pace to top speed, with your threshold pace in the middle of the key. Laps and out-and-backs blend, and the fast running always shows on top.
  • Speed points Each run is scored for the ground you gained running faster than threshold pace. A set of strides counts; an easy run doesn't.
  • Speed development, week by week Your weekly speed load against what your legs are used to (the last four weeks). Build steadily and see when a week jumps too far past it.
  • Threshold pace that keeps up with you Worked out from your own hardest efforts and updated as they improve, with every rep and stride picked out of the run automatically.
  • Ask about any run "How did the reps go?" Dieter answers from the run itself, and works with Claude and ChatGPT.
  • Coach mode A coach writes "6 × mile at threshold" in plain words; Dieter fills in each athlete's paces.
